About Julia

Finding Stillness in the Fire

Glass requires total presence. You cannot rush molten light. Over years of mastering the craft, shaping fragile materials became Julia's emotional anchor, turning daily stress into deep, lasting calm.

“You don’t force glass into shape—you listen to how it breathes under the flame.”

She established Unbreakable Ju to share that quiet refuge. Step away from city distractions for a few mindful hours, unwind your thoughts, and shape your own piece of peace to take home.

— Julia, Founder & Artisan
The Craftsmanship

Two Ways to Experience the Fire

Glass Blowing • Hot Shop

Get Ready to Be Blown Away

Glass blowing manipulates molten glass directly from a 1,100°C furnace. Learn pipe gathering, bubble formation, and shaping techniques to build your creative expression in a fully supervised, high-energy environment.

Flame Working • Bench Torch

Get Fired Up for Flame Working

At our flame working bench, gas torches melt and fuse colorful borosilicate glass rods. Create delicate miniature sculptures, custom glass charms, stirrers, and detailed ornaments with precise gravity manipulation.
